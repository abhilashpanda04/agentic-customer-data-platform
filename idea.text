New customers.

At-risk high-value customers.

Hibernating customers.

Low-value or one-time buyers.

5. Lookalike modeling
Select a seed audience, for example:

Top 10% by predicted CLTV.

Customers with repeat purchases.

Customers with high contribution margin.

Customers retained after a win-back campaign.

Train a binary classifier to distinguish seed customers from the broader customer base. Rank non-seed customers by similarity or predicted probability.

Evaluate:

Precision in the top 1%, 5%, and 10%.

Lift against random sampling.

Average predicted CLTV of the selected audience.

Conversion rate of the lookalike audience.

Fairness and exclusion rules.

Do not use sensitive attributes. Add business constraints such as consent status, country eligibility, channel eligibility, and existing-customer suppression.

Implement two matching strategies:
